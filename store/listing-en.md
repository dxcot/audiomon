## Full description (max 4000)

No speakers on your computer? audiomon plays everything your Windows PC plays (videos, music, games) through your Android phone.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Install the free audiomon app for Windows on your PC (download it from github.com/dxcot/audiomon).
2. Open audiomon on your phone.
3. That's it: the phone and the PC find each other by themselves.

TWO WAYS TO CONNECT
• Wi-Fi: keep the phone and the PC on the same network. Nothing else to set up.
• USB cable, for the lowest delay: plug the phone into the PC and turn on USB tethering in your phone's settings. audiomon switches to the cable automatically.

LOW DELAY, SO VIDEOS STAY IN SYNC
audiomon measures how steady your connection is and keeps only as much audio buffered as it needs. Over the USB cable the total delay is about 25–30 ms on our reference setup, low enough for videos and most games. Wi-Fi works too, with a bit more delay depending on your network.

CLEAR AT A GLANCE
The screen shows whether sound is playing, over which connection, and the current delay. Open Details to see where the delay comes from.

LIGHT ON YOUR BATTERY
audiomon is built to play for hours with the screen locked. It uses Android's low-latency audio output and spends no power on animations.

PRIVATE
Your audio goes only from your PC to your phone, on your own cable or network. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data collected.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
audiomon is free, has no ads, and its code is public: github.com/dxcot/audiomon

Requirements: Android 9 or newer; Windows 10 or 11 PC with the audiomon Windows app.
